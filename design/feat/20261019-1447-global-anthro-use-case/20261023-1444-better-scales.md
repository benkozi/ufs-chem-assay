## Goal

1. **Log by default.** Every overview map (`plots-overview/`) of a variable uses a logarithmic color scale unless the suite says otherwise: `plotting.scale: log`. The data this harness plots — emission fluxes and concentrations — are non-negative, mostly zero or near zero, and span many orders of magnitude; a linear scale shows a purple globe with a handful of bright cells (the maccity CO map today), a log scale shows the field.
2. **Configurable, including none.** `plotting.scale` takes `log`, `symlog`, or `linear` (`linear` is today's behaviour, byte for byte: no transform); the scale's **floor** — where the log axis starts — is derived from the suite-wide statistics with a configurable decade span (`plotting.decades`, default 5 below the suite-wide maximum) and can be pinned explicitly (`plotting.floor`, in data units). Bias maps (`plots-baselines/`) keep their symmetric linear scale by default and gain `plotting.bias_scale: linear | symlog`.
3. **Zeros are a value, not noise.** Under `log`, cells with zero (or negative) values are masked and drawn in a neutral grey distinct from the lowest color, and the colorbar label says so: the map then distinguishes "no emission" from "small emission", which the linear map could not.
4. **Still suite-wide and still exact at the top.** The maximum stays the exact suite-wide maximum from the stats (an outlier that saturates a plot is a finding — the module's standing rule); only the floor is a choice, because on a log axis the exact smallest positive value (1e-19 in the maccity data, ten decades below the maximum) would spend most of the colorbar on numerically negligible values. The stats gain `min_positive` so the derived floor is suite-wide and exact too.

## Research: scaling options for these fields (2026-10-23)

**The data.** The maccity CO flux (`co`, kg m⁻² s⁻¹, 72×46 cells, three hourly files): 46.8 % of cells are exactly zero (ocean, no inventory), the positive values run from 7.6e-19 to 5.3e-9 — **9.8 decades** — with the median positive at 3.9e-13 and the 99th percentile at 9.0e-10. The gcafs-anthro fields (SO2, OC, BC on a 0.25° global grid, CEDS sectors summed with hourly/weekly/daily weights) have the same shape: zero over most of the ocean, a long tail of tiny values where a coarse inventory was regridded onto a fine grid, and a few decades of meaningful signal concentrated in populated land. A linear colorbar from 0 to the max puts 99 % of the positive cells in the bottom 2 % of the colormap.

**Matplotlib's options** (3.11 is pinned):

| norm | handles zero | shape | parameters | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none (`vmin`/`vmax`) | yes | linear | none | today; hides the field |
| `LogNorm` | no — ≤ 0 is masked ("bad" color) | log₁₀ over `[vmin, vmax]`, `vmin > 0` | the floor | **the default**: the convention for emission and concentration maps (HEMCO, MONET, the inventory papers); zeros become an explicit "no emission" class |
| `SymLogNorm` | yes — linear within `±linthresh`, log outside | log with a linear core | `linthresh`, `linscale` | the option when zeros must sit on the colorbar; also the right bias-map alternative (signed data) |
| `AsinhNorm` | yes — smooth, linear near 0, log beyond `linear_width` | arcsinh | `linear_width` | smoother than symlog, same parameter burden, less familiar on a colorbar; not offered |
| `PowerNorm(γ)` | yes | `x^γ` | `γ` | compresses but cannot span ten decades; not offered |
| percentile clipping | — | linear on a clipped range | the percentiles | rejected by the module's rule (exactness; an outlier is a finding) |

**The floor.** A log axis must start somewhere positive. Three candidates, measured on maccity: the exact suite-wide smallest positive (7.6e-19: ten decades, the signal squeezed into the top third), a fixed number of decades below the maximum, or a user-pinned value in data units (the right answer when a field has a known detection floor). The design offers all three: `decades` as the default rule, `floor` as the override, `decades: null` for the exact minimum positive. **Rendered side by side** (the maccity CO field at 4, 5, 6 and 7 decades, log scale, zeros grey — the user asked for the best visual, 2026-10-23): at 4 the continents saturate and the low ocean cells merge into the floor; at 6 and 7 the land gradient flattens because the colorbar spends itself on the near-zero ocean tail; **5 decades** (`[5e-14, 5e-9]`) keeps the land structure crisp, the shipping lanes and low cells visible, and the grey zeros distinct — the default. Values between zero and the floor are **clipped** to the lowest color (they are still "some emission"), zeros are **masked** (grey): two different statements on the map.

**Bias maps.** The difference field is signed and centred on zero; its magnitude is the comparison's finding (one float64 ULP for maccity across builds, `rtol 1e-15`). A symmetric linear scale at the exact max |diff| is honest and stays the default; `symlog` (linthresh = the same decade rule on max |diff|) is offered for the case where a few cells differ grossly and the rest differ by ULPs — the log tail shows the structure of the small differences.

## Design

### 0. Decisions

1. **`log` is the default for the overview maps.** Non-negative fields spanning many decades are what the harness plots; the linear default was inherited from the viewer, not chosen. Existing suites get log maps on their next run — plots are session-end diagnostics, never compared, never part of a baseline's verdict, so the change breaks nothing.
2. **Three scales, one knob.** `plotting.scale: log | symlog | linear`. Not `AsinhNorm` (an extra parameter with no familiar colorbar) and not `PowerNorm` (cannot span the range); both can be added as values later without touching the model's shape.
3. **The floor is a rule with an override, never a percentile.** `plotting.decades: 5` (the floor is `vmax / 10**decades`, raised to the suite-wide smallest positive value when that is larger, so the axis never starts below real data); `plotting.decades: null` means the exact smallest positive value; `plotting.floor: <float>` pins it in data units and wins. `vmax` is always the exact suite-wide maximum.
4. **Zeros are masked under `log`, clipped values are not.** `LogNorm` masks `≤ 0` as "bad": drawn in `#d9d9d9` (viridis' lowest color is dark purple, so the two never read alike) and named in the colorbar label (`co [kg m-2 s-1] (grey: 0)`). Values in `(0, floor)` are clipped to the lowest color by the norm, as matplotlib does. Under `symlog` zeros sit inside the linear core and nothing is masked.
5. **`min_positive` joins the statistics.** `VariableStats.min_positive: float | None` (the smallest value > 0; None when none) — one more NaN-aware reduction in the dask pipeline, one more CSV column, so the floor rule has an exact suite-wide input and `derive_scales` keeps its "no NetCDF re-read" property. A stats CSV without the column (a run before this change) derives scales with `min_positive = None`, which falls back to the decade rule alone.
6. **Bias maps default to linear, offer symlog.** `plotting.bias_scale: linear | symlog`, `plotting.bias_decades: 5`; symmetric about zero either way at the exact max |diff|; `symlog`'s `linthresh = vmax / 10**bias_decades`.
7. **The scale is recorded.** `VariableScale` gains `scale` and `floor`; the log line at scale derivation names them (`color scale for co: log [5.25e-14, 5.25e-09], floor by 5 decades`), and the colorbar title carries the scale name when it is not linear.
8. **GIFs, file names, directories: unchanged.** A viewer of `plots-overview/co.gif` sees the same frames with a different norm.

### 1. The suite model — `models/suite_config.py`

```python
@unique
class ColorScale(StrEnum):
    log = "log"
    symlog = "symlog"
    linear = "linear"

@unique
class BiasColorScale(StrEnum):
    linear = "linear"
    symlog = "symlog"

class Plotting(StrictModel):
    enabled: bool = True
    gif_enabled: bool = True
    scale: ColorScale = Field(ColorScale.log, description="Overview-map color scale: log (zeros masked grey, the default), symlog (zeros on the colorbar), or linear (no transform)")
    decades: int | None = Field(5, ge=1, description="log/symlog: the floor is the suite-wide max / 10**decades, never below the suite-wide smallest positive value; null = that smallest positive value exactly")
    floor: float | None = Field(None, gt=0, description="log/symlog: an explicit floor in data units; overrides decades")
    bias_scale: BiasColorScale = Field(BiasColorScale.linear, description="Bias-map color scale: linear (symmetric at the exact max |diff|, the default) or symlog")
    bias_decades: int = Field(5, ge=1, description="bias symlog: linthresh = max |diff| / 10**bias_decades")
```

`floor` with `scale: linear` is accepted and ignored (documented), so switching scales is one line.

### 2. The statistics — `analysis.py`

`VariableStats.min_positive: float | None` (description: "smallest value > 0; null when the field has none"), computed beside `min`/`max` in the same dask reduction (`where(values > 0)` then `nanmin`; NaN → None), written as a column after `min`. `concatenate_stats_csvs` is unchanged. The per-combo and session CSVs gain the column; `test_analysis.py` pins it (a field with zeros, a field with none).

### 3. The scales — `plotting.py`

```python
class VariableScale(BaseModel):
    variable: str
    vmin: float            # the floor under log/symlog; the exact min under linear
    vmax: float            # always the exact suite-wide maximum
    scale: ColorScale | BiasColorScale
    floor: float | None    # the log/symlog floor as chosen (None for linear)

def derive_scales(stats, plotting: Plotting) -> dict[str, VariableScale]
def derive_bias_scales(comparison_stats, plotting: Plotting) -> dict[str, VariableScale]
def make_norm(scale: VariableScale) -> Normalize | None
```

- `derive_scales`: `vmax` exact; for `log`/`symlog` the floor is `plotting.floor` if set, else `vmax / 10**decades` raised to `min_positive` when the column is present and larger, else (`decades: null`) `min_positive`; a variable with no positive value (all zero) or a non-finite floor **falls back to linear for that variable with a WARNING** (nothing to take the log of). `linear` keeps today's `vmin`/`vmax` (and the ±0.5 degenerate guard).
- `make_norm`: `LogNorm(vmin=floor, vmax)`, `SymLogNorm(linthresh=floor, vmin=0 or -vmax, vmax, base=10)`, or `None` (linear → `vmin`/`vmax` passed as today).
- `render_spatial_plot`: under `log`, `data = np.ma.masked_less_equal(data, 0)`, a copied viridis with `set_bad("#d9d9d9")`, `pcolormesh(..., norm=make_norm(scale))` (no `vmin`/`vmax` when a norm is given — matplotlib forbids both); the colorbar label gains `(grey: 0)`; the title gains ` [log]` / ` [symlog]`. The linear path is byte-identical to today.
- `render_bias_plot`: `norm=SymLogNorm(linthresh=floor, vmin=-vmax, vmax=vmax)` for `symlog`, today's `vmin`/`vmax` for linear.
- The session (`conftest.pytest_sessionfinish`) passes `context.suite.plotting` to both entry points; `render_all_plots(output_root, stats, plotting)` replaces the `gif_enabled` argument (it is in `plotting`).

### 5. Edge cases and guards

- **All-zero field** (a species with no emission anywhere, or a dry-ish test fixture): `min_positive` null, log impossible → linear for that variable with a warning; the degenerate ±0.5 guard still applies.
- **`floor` above `vmax`** (a mis-set override): refused at scale derivation with a clear error for that variable (fall back to the decade rule, WARNING), never a crash of the session-end hook (plots are wrapped already).
- **Negative values** in an overview field (a difference-like diagnostic the driver might write): masked under `log` like zeros, in the linear core under `symlog`, plain under `linear`.
- **A suite slice whose stats predate the column**: `derive_scales` tolerates a missing `min_positive` column (treated as null).
- **NaN cells**: masked by pcolormesh already; unchanged.
- **Colorbar ticks under `LogNorm`** are decade ticks with `10^n` labels; `SymLogNorm` shows the linear core as a short segment — both matplotlib defaults, kept.

## Risks and notes

- **Colorbar readability**: log ticks at `10^-15 … 10^-9` are what emission maps show; the `(grey: 0)` note is the one unusual element and is spelled out in the label.
- **`decades: 5` is a judgement**, made by looking (the research section's side-by-side render of the maccity field). A user with a field of narrower range sees a floor below the data — harmless (the floor is raised to `min_positive`). A field with a wider meaningful range sets `decades` higher or `floor` directly.
- **Published baselines' plots** are as rendered at publish time; re-publishing re-renders. Baselines are not compared by their plots.
- **Performance**: no change — the norm is applied at draw time; `min_positive` is one more reduction over data already loaded.
